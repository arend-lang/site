title:  "Arend 1.12.0 released"
---

This release includes a major internal overhaul of the universe/level system, an opt-in binary caching mode for the CLI,
and substantial additions to arend-lib, most notably in algebraic geometry and analysis.

Language updates:

* Introduce [types not bounded by universe levels](/documentation/language-reference/expressions/universes).
* Introduce classes not bounded by universe levels, with a local override available when a definition needs to be pinned to a finite level.
* Prefix application of infix operators is now forbidden.
  Applying an infix operator to a single argument is instead interpreted as a right section.

Plugin updates:

* Updated IntelliJ platform support to 2025.3, then to 2026.2.
* Added basic Arend support for the JetBrains Educational plugin (course builder, project generator, and task checker).
* Fixed a UI freeze in Find Usages, formatter performance issues, and the Arend Messages panel.

CLI updates:

* Added an opt-in binary cache (`.arc`) serialization mode for faster incremental rechecking:
  pass `--serialize` to persist typechecked results, and `-r`/`--recompile` to ignore existing caches.
* Added console query tooling: `-ss` (symbol search), `-ps` ([proof search](/documentation/proof-search), previously IDE-only),
  `-fu` (find usages), `-ch` (class hierarchy), and `-sc` (scope), all with `--json` output support.
* REPL improvements: Ctrl+J (multi-line input), Ctrl+C (clear line), Ctrl+D (quit), `-L` support, and module preloading.
* Added support for typechecking individual definitions from the CLI.

arend-lib:

* Introduce [scheme sites](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/AG/SchemeSite.html),
  [affine opens](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/AG/RingedLocale.html),
  [affine schemes](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/AG/AffineScheme.html),
  and [general schemes](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/AG/Scheme.html) built from a scheme site.
* Construct [Spec](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/AG/AffineScheme.html)
  and [Proj](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/AG/Projective.html);
  prove Spec is a [reflective embedding](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/AG/AffineScheme/SpecFunctor.html)
  of CRing^op into scheme sites and check the [sheaf condition](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/AG/SchemeSite.html)
  for the structure sheaf on Spec R.
* Define [locally ringed locales](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/AG/RingedLocale.html)
  and construct a [fully faithful functor](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/AG/SchemeSite/SchemeEquiv.html)
  and [equivalence of morphisms](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/AG/SchemeSite/LRLSchemeSiteHom.html) between scheme sites
  and locally ringed locales; prove [every scheme can be reconstructed](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/AG/SchemeSite/SchemeEquiv.html)
  from a scheme site.
* Refactor [locales](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Topology/Locale.html)
  and [sites](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Category/Topos/Sheaf/Site.html),
  including [dense sites](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Category/Topos/Sheaf/DenseExtend.html)
  and [preorder sites](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Topology/Locale/PreorderSite.html);
  prove the [category of ringed locales](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/AG/RingedLocale/RingedLocaleCat.html) is univalent.
* [Cauchy product of power series](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Analysis/CauchyProduct.html);
  make [exp](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Arith/Exp.html)
  and [log(1+x)](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Arith/Log.html) into functions with full analytic properties;
  define [sin and cos](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Arith/Trig/Real.html)
  and prove [cos π = -1](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Arith/Real/Pi.html).
* Make [complex numbers a Banach algebra](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Arith/Complex/Banach.html).
* Define [Noetherian rings](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Algebra/Ring/Noetherian.html)
  and prove [products of Noetherian rings are Noetherian](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Algebra/Ring/Noetherian/Examples.html);
  define Bar-Noetherian rings, [bar induction](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Logic/Bar.html),
  and prove the [fan theorem](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Logic/Bar/FanTheorem.html).
* Prove [Hilbert's basis theorem](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Algebra/Ring/Noetherian/HilbertBasisTheorem.html)
  for Bar-Noetherian rings.
* Define tensor products of [modules](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Algebra/Module/TensorProduct.html)
  and of [algebras](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Algebra/Algebra/TensorProduct.html).
* Refactor [Heyting algebras](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Order/HeytingAlgebra.html),
  [complete lattices](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Order/Lattice/CompleteLattice.html),
  and the localization of [rings](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Algebra/Ring/Localization.html)
  and [monoids](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Algebra/Monoid/Localization.html).
* Replace Equiv with [QEquiv and IsEquiv](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Equiv.html) throughout the library.
* Refactor the hierarchy of [substructures](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Set/SubSet.html)
  and [group representation theory](/arend-lib/v1.12.0/arend-html-files/src/Algebra/Group/Representation.html).

  extending the [linarith](/documentation/standard-tactics/algebra-meta#linarith)-style solver,
  including Nat div/mod support.
